Prohibition on unsolicited messages

This web site, pathtoqualify.com and go.pathtoqualify.com, may not be advertised or promoted through unsolicited messages of any kind, by anyone. We operate no affiliate program, no referral-for-payment program, and no reseller arrangement. No third party is authorized to send any message advertising this site. Any message advertising this site that was not sent by Griselda Flores is unauthorized, and we ask that it be reported to us at the address below.

Who sends from this domain

Mail from pathtoqualify.com is sent by Griselda Flores, the only sender, who signs mail as Gigi. This domain does not host, resell, or forward mail for anyone else.

How addresses are obtained

We do not purchase, rent, trade, or bulk-harvest address lists, and we do not accept lists from third parties. We do not contact consumers and we do not mail personal addresses. We do not contact generic or role addresses found on a web page with no named individual attached.

How we send

Low volume, individually addressed, authenticated with SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Every message identifies the sender by name, states its purpose plainly, and carries an opt-out instruction.

Opting out

Reply STOP or write to gigi@pathtoqualify.com. Removal is immediate and permanent, requires no explanation, and requires nothing further from you. Removed addresses are retained only on a suppression list so they are never contacted again.

Reporting abuse

gigi@pathtoqualify.com. Reports are read by the owner, answered, and acted on. If a message from this domain reached an address that should not have been contacted, tell us the address and it is removed permanently the same day.
