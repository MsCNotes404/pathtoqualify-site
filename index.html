A straightforward process

What happens after you introduce a buyer

The buyer gets a careful review. You get appropriate progress updates, with the buyer's permission.

01

We hear their situation

We learn what held up the loan and review the buyer's credit reports. If there is nothing appropriate for us to address, we say so.

02

We explain a plan

We identify information that may be inaccurate or incomplete and explain the steps available to address it.

03

We keep you informed

With the buyer's permission, you can follow the file's status and reconnect when they are ready to revisit financing.

What you can expect

A candid answer is part of the service.

We won't tell every buyer that every account can be removed. We review each situation, explain what we find, and set expectations before work begins.

  • Clear explanation of what we can and cannot address
  • Documented work on credit-report issues where appropriate
  • Progress visibility only with the buyer's permission
